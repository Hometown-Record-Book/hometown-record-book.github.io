Built for coaches

Add a game in a few minutes

No spreadsheets to fix and nothing to install. Your coach signs in on the site with a PIN and adds each game from a laptop or a phone.

1

Sign in and set up the season

Click Coach login at the top of your site and type the PIN. Only your staff has it.

Starting a new season? Paste in the roster and the schedule from a spreadsheet, MaxPreps or the school website.

2

Bring in the stats

After a game, type the score by quarter and the final adds itself up. Then pick the way that suits you for the player stats:

  • The MaxPreps file from Statman or Hudl
  • Copy and paste from Arbiter Live, Statman or a spreadsheet
  • Type them in, like a stat sheet
3

Check the numbers

The stats show up like a box score, player by player. The site checks that the points from the player stats match the final score before you save.

4

Save, and you're done

The box score, the season and player pages, and the record book update right away. New records get a NEW badge so fans can spot them.

Questions

Good to know

Is it just for football?

No. Football is shown here, but we build record books for basketball, baseball, volleyball and every other sport, or one site for your whole school. Email us and we'll get you a quote.

Who adds the games during the season?

Your coach or a stats person, with the PIN. Paste the stats from Arbiter Live, Statman or a spreadsheet, choose the MaxPreps file, or type them in. It works on a phone.

Do we need any software or a server?

No. Your site runs on Google, and your stats are kept in a Google Sheet. There's nothing to install.

Who owns our stats?

Your school does. Ask any time and we'll send you a full copy.

What if our old records are on paper?

Send us what you have, even photos or scans, and we'll give you a quote for typing it in.

Can fans and alumni see it?

Yes. Anyone can open your link on a phone or computer. Only your staff can sign in to add or change games.

What does the yearly fee cover?

Keeping your site online and working, help for your coach when something comes up, and new features as we add them.
